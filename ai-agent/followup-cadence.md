# 🔄 SHAKTIX 5-Stage Automated Follow-Up Cadence

*Never let an interested lead slip away. Most clients don't say NO — they are just busy.*

---

### ⏱️ Timeline Overview
* **Day 0**: Initial Pitch / Free Mini-Audit sent.
* **Day 2 (+48 hours)**: Casual check-in with screenshot / demo feature.
* **Day 4 (+96 hours)**: Social proof & competitor comparison.
* **Day 7 (1 Week)**: Special limited-time launch offer (20% off or free SEO setup).
* **Day 14 (2 Weeks)**: The "Breakup" message (Triggers FOMO and gets 25% response).

---

### 📩 Sequence Scripts

#### Day 2: Friendly Follow-Up
> *"Namaste [Name] ji! 🙏*
> *Bas check karna tha ki kya aapne upar bheja gaya demo link check karne ka time mila?*
> *Agar koi questions hain design ya features ke baare me, toh main WhatsApp par live bata sakta hoon."*

#### Day 4: Social Proof & Feature Highlight
> *"Hey [Name]! 👋*
> *Aapko ek quick update dena tha — humne is demo template me ek new feature add kiya hai: **1-Click WhatsApp Enquiry with Auto-Filled Message**.*
> *Isse patient/customer ko type bhi nahi karna padta aur direct lead aapke WhatsApp par aa jati hai.*
> *Kya aap chahte hain ki hum aapke business ke liye iska ek personalized mockup share karein?"*

#### Day 7: Limited Opportunity Offer
> *"Namaste [Name] ji! 🙏*
> *Hum is mahine aapke area ([Locality/City]) me 2 selected businesses ko **SHAKTIX Growth Partner Program** me onboard kar rahe hain.*
> *Isme hum **₹2,500 ki Google SEO & Speed Optimization bilkul FREE** provide kar rahe hain.*
> *Agar aap interested hain, toh kya hum aaj sham 10 minute discuss kar sakte hain?"*

#### Day 14: The Polite Breakup Message
> *"Namaste [Name] ji! 🙏*
> *Lagta hai ki abhi aapke business ke liye new website top priority nahi hai, totally understand!*
> *Main aage se follow-up messages bhej kar aapko disturb nahi karunga.*
> *Agar future me kabhi bhi Google par leads badhani ho ya premium website banwani ho, toh mera number save rakh sakte hain.*
> *Wishing [Business Name] tremendous success! 🚀"*
