# 🔍 SHAKTIX 2-Minute Digital Audit Framework

*The "Trojan Horse" technique: Instead of sending a pushy sales pitch, send a free, helpful mini-audit. This generates a 3x to 5x higher response rate.*

---

## 📋 The 4-Point Mini Audit Checklist
When looking at any business on Google Maps or Instagram, check these 4 points:

1. **Mobile Responsiveness & Load Speed:**
   - Does their page take more than 3 seconds to load?
   - Is text tiny on mobile screens?
2. **Missing Direct CTA (Call-to-Action):**
   - Can a visitor book an appointment or order in 1 click via WhatsApp?
   - Or do they force the visitor to copy a 10-digit number and dial manually?
3. **Google Maps & Local SEO Optimization:**
   - Is the business category exact?
   - Is there a direct link from Google Maps to an official appointment page?
4. **Trust Signals:**
   - Are doctor degrees, gym trainer certifications, customer testimonials prominently displayed with photo verification?
