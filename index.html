Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about our web development and delivery process.

How fast can my website go live?

For standard Express Launch and Business Growth packages, your site is fully coded, reviewed, and deployed live within 48 to 72 hours once you share your basic content and business details.

What is included in the 1-Year Free Support (AMC)?

You receive high-speed NVMe cloud hosting, SSL certificates, regular security patches, text/image content updates, daily cloud backups, and direct WhatsApp tech support from our team.

How does the 50% milestone payment policy work?

You pay a 50% advance to start the project. Once the complete live demo is created on our preview server and you are 100% satisfied with the design and features, you pay the remaining 50% before final domain handover.

Will my website rank on Google Search?

Yes. Every website we build includes semantic HTML5 tags, mobile responsiveness, Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and canonical URLs engineered for Google indexing.

Do I get full ownership of my website and code?

Yes, 100%. You receive complete ownership of all website code, assets, domains, and database credentials without any recurring vendor lock-in fees.
